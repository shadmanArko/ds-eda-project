What counts as "middle-class"?

Recommendations for Bonnie

Six moves, ordered by impact.

Price by her exact zip code

Not the "middle-class" label as a whole — a 28% swing lives inside that one tier.

Skip pure cosmetic fixes

Condition showed no reliable price relationship — that budget is better spent elsewhere.

A genuine renovation, if time allows

The one improvement lever with a real, sizeable payoff in this data.

Add space, not bedroom count

Usable square footage moved price far more than an extra bedroom on its own.

List in spring, if flexible

A modest lever — but free, and it brings the most competing buyers.

Check for any water view

The single biggest price factor found — if it applies, it changes the whole plan.
